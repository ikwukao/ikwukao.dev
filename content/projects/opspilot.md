## Overview

OpsPilot is a DevOps platform built to simplify infrastructure automation, operational visibility, and developer workflows.

The project focuses on creating reliable tooling that helps engineers automate repetitive operational tasks while improving deployment consistency and developer productivity.

---

## Motivation

Modern infrastructure management often involves repetitive manual processes.

OpsPilot was created to explore how developer tooling, automation, and cloud-native practices can simplify day-to-day operations while serving as a practical learning project in backend engineering and DevOps.
