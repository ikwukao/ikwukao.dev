Background

Security taught me to think about failure. Engineering taught me to design for it.

My engineering journey began in cybersecurity, where I learned to think deeply about systems, failure modes, infrastructure, and the consequences of poor design.

That perspective naturally pulled me toward backend engineering and distributed systems. I became increasingly interested in what happens beneath the interface — how services communicate, how data moves, how systems recover, and how infrastructure supports reliable software.

Today, I focus on building backend services, distributed systems, developer tooling, and cloud-native infrastructure with Go and the broader systems engineering ecosystem.
