My journey into software engineering began with cybersecurity, where I developed a strong appreciation for system reliability, infrastructure, and problem-solving under real-world constraints.

As my interests evolved, I shifted toward backend engineering and distributed systems—fields that combine software design, scalability, automation, and infrastructure.

Today, I focus on building backend applications, distributed services, developer tools, and cloud-native solutions while continuously learning modern software architecture and systems engineering.
