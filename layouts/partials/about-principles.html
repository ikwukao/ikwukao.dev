01

Reliability First

Software should remain dependable under real-world conditions, not merely work when everything goes right.

02

Simplicity Scales

Prefer understandable systems over unnecessary abstraction and cleverness.

03

Automate the Repetitive

Engineering time is better spent solving meaningful problems than repeatedly performing predictable tasks.

04

Observe Before Optimizing

Measure system behavior first. Optimize based on evidence rather than assumptions.

05

Design for Change

Maintainability matters because the systems we build today will evolve tomorrow.

06

Build for People

Good engineering ultimately exists to make products, teams, and users more effective.
