Reliability First

Software should be dependable before it is clever.

Keep It Simple

Simple systems are easier to maintain and scale.

Automate Everything

Eliminate repetitive work with automation.

Observability Matters

Systems should explain what they're doing.

Continuous Learning

Engineering is a lifelong process of improvement.

Build for People

Great software improves the lives of its users and developers.
