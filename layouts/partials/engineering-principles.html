Reliability over cleverness.

Prefer predictable, resilient systems over unnecessary complexity or clever abstractions.

Simplicity scales.

Keep systems understandable so they remain easier to operate, extend, and debug as they grow.

Automate repetitive work.

Turn repeatable engineering tasks into reliable automation that reduces friction and human error.

Measure before optimizing.

Use observability, profiling, and evidence to identify real bottlenecks before changing the system.

Design for maintainability.

Write software that another engineer can understand, operate, test, and safely change.

Documentation is part of engineering.

Capture important decisions, system behavior, and operational knowledge as part of the engineering process.
